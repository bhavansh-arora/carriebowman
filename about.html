(03)Carrie’s storyIn her words

From outside-in to inside-out.

“I spent the first three decades of my life seeking external solutions to correct an internal problem. The equation I had relied upon was faulty.”

01
Seeking

Goal after goal after goal.

Raised in a culture that glorifies external solutions, Carrie believed that if she made the outside of her life perfect enough, happiness would follow. Each goal was supposed to be “enough”. One more change had to be the one. It never was.

02
Having it all

Degrees. Career. Freedom.

“To the outside observer, I had it all. But deep inside, I felt disconnected and alone.”

So she tried harder: more money, more degrees, marriage, the best shape of her life. Nothing she achieved made a shred of difference. Nothing brought her peace.

03
Rock bottom

Then came a new friend: vodka.

Untreated alcoholism began to affect every part of her life. Friends and family distanced themselves. She was in and out of hospitals, couldn’t hold down a job, and her finances ran out. Her future, if she had one, looked bleak.

04
The turn

A friend’s offer of grace.

At her last visit to the emergency room, a friend came to offer help, if she was willing to take a few simple steps. There was no judgment in it, only love. Something shifted. Carrie held on to what her friend believed until she believed it too.

“For many of my clients, that’s what I do. I hold the vision of who they truly are until they can step into it themselves.”
05
The choice

Total destruction or total transformation.

Two comprehensive rehab stays and deep investment in her own healing later, two things became shockingly clear. She desperately wanted to live. And she could never go back to the way things were.

06
Inside-out

The answers were never outside.

Recovery became a journey of self-discovery, heightened consciousness and a realigned purpose. Living from the inside out, she found an open space within, from which to create and prosper. That space exists in you, too.

07
The mission

Helping you find your answers.

Carrie made it her mission to help people connect with the answers they already hold, so they can radically transform their lives by experiencing a different version of what’s possible.

“Your life is too precious to let your days slip numbly by. You deserve to wake up every day completely in love with your life.”
