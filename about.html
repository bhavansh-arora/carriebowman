(03)Carrie’s storyIn her words

From outside-in to inside-out.

“I spent the first three decades of my life seeking external solutions to correct an internal problem. The equation I had relied upon was faulty.”

01Chapter 1 of 7Seeking
01
Seeking

Goal after goal after goal.

Make the outside perfect enough and happiness would follow. Each goal was supposed to be “enough”. It never was.

02
Having it all

Degrees. Career. Freedom.

“To the outside observer, I had it all. But deep inside, I felt disconnected and alone.”
03
Rock bottom

Then came a new friend: vodka.

Untreated alcoholism reached every part of her life: family, work, health and money.

04
The turn

A friend’s offer of grace.

No judgment, only love. Carrie held on to what her friend believed until she believed it too.

“For many of my clients, that’s what I do. I hold the vision of who they truly are until they can step into it themselves.”
05
The choice

Total destruction or total transformation.

She desperately wanted to live, and she could never go back.

06
Inside-out

The answers were never outside.

Recovery became self-discovery, and an open space within to create from. That space exists in you, too.
