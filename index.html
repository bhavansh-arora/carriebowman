(02)Why nothing has stuckIt’s not your fault

Stop thinking about change. Experience it.

For every problem, someone is selling a solution. Most of them only engage your thinking brain, so you think about change instead of living it. The moment stress hits, the old patterns come straight back. That’s not a flaw in you. It’s how our brains are wired.

The usual fixes

Thinking about change

  • Self-help books by self-proclaimed gurus
  • Podcasts, meditation apps and productivity hacks
  • Talk therapy that’s insightful, but stays in the conversation
  • Coaching that fixes one area while another pops up
vs
Carrie’s approach

Experiencing change

  • Your brain: neuroscience and clinical psychology
  • Your body: stress physiology and somatic regulation
  • Your emotions: processed, not just talked about
  • Your inner self: purpose, values and real action

This isn’t about embracing the “woo”. The skills and strategies Carrie teaches are grounded in brain science, and they reach every part of your life: your work, your relationships and the way you see yourself.
